### 📄 Project Brief

> FitTrack is a fitness tracking app designed to help users build consistent workout habits. The app targets 18–35 year olds who want a simple, no-friction way to log workouts, track progress, and stay motivated. This project covers the full lifecycle from discovery to App Store launch across iOS and Android platforms.

### 🎯 Success Metrics

- 10,000 downloads in the first 30 days
- 4.0+ App Store rating at launch
- 40% D7 retention rate
- Launch on time — within 6-month timeline
